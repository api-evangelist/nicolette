Nicolette, Inc. has launched NicoBoard, its bedside empowerment technology for parents with babies in the neonatal intensive care unit (NICU). Parents use NicoBoard to view updates and insights about their baby’s health, receive curated education specific to their baby, and document their NICU journey with sharable media and journaling.
